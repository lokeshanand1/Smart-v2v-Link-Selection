# Research References

This project and the associated paper draw upon established research in wireless communication, optical propagation, and machine learning.

## Key Standards
- **ITU-R P.838-3:** Specific attenuation model for rain for use in prediction methods.
- **ITU-R P.676:** Attenuation by atmospheric gases and related effects.

## Vehicular Communication & Propagation
- **Fog Attenuation Model:** Based on the Kim Model for atmospheric scattering in Visible Light Communication.
- **VLC Vehicular Communication:** Research regarding Line-of-Sight (LOS) probability and optical channel gain in dynamic V2V environments.

## Machine Learning & Optimization
- **Random Forest:** Breiman, L. (2001). "Random Forests". Machine Learning, 45, 5-32.
- **XGBoost:** Chen, T., & Guestrin, C. (2016). "XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System". KDD '16.
- **Utility Optimization:** Multi-objective utility theory applied to network selection and resource allocation in heterogeneous networks.
